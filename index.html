PERSONAL AUTOMATION · EDunaev

n8n Personal

A private, self-hosted automation tool operated by the GitHub user EDunaev on a Raspberry Pi, for the owner's own Google account.

Current Gmail functions

The workflows look up Gmail labels and mark selected messages as read by removing their UNREAD label. Google OAuth is used to obtain the account owner's permission.

This information website does not provide access to the automation service or a public Google sign-in.

Planned functions

Reading and sending email, AI processing, and Telegram integration are planned. The privacy policy will be updated to identify the data flows and recipients before these features are enabled.
